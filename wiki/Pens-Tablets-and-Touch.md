# Pens, tablets and touch

Dead Pixel works with a mouse, a pen or a finger on PCs, tablets and phones.

## Pen pressure

On the pencil, eraser and shading tools, the option bar's **Pen pressure** setting chooses what pressing harder
does:

- **Size**: the brush grows from **Min** at the lightest touch up to the brush size. Set the size above 1 to feel it.
- **Dither density**: a light touch paints a sparse 4×4 pattern and a hard press paints solid. Use this for effects
  layers; the defaults keep dither out of the art itself.
- **Off**: pressure does nothing.

Pressure works with pens that report it: Apple Pencil (Safari on iPad), Surface, Wacom and other Windows Ink pens
(Chrome, Edge, Firefox), and Samsung S Pen (Chrome on Android).

## Pen buttons

- The pen's **eraser end** erases, whatever tool is chosen.
- The **side (barrel) button** paints with the secondary colour.

## Fingers

- Pinch with two fingers to zoom and pan.
- Tap with two fingers to undo, and with three to redo.
- Once you've used a pen, one finger pans instead of painting, so a resting palm doesn't leave marks.

## Touch layout

On touch screens the buttons get bigger and the toolbox gains Undo and Redo buttons. On phones the toolbox, option
bar and panels stack.

## Preferences

**View > Preferences** holds these settings. They are kept in this browser.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Pen pressure changes** | Brush size, dither density, or nothing. |
| **Smallest size** | The brush size at the lightest touch (1 to 64). |
| **Pressure curve** | Soft (a light touch goes far), linear, or firm (press harder). |
| **One finger** | Paints until a pen is used, then pans (auto); always paints; or always pans. |
| **Two-finger tap undo, three-finger tap redo** | On or off. |
| **Undo memory (MB)** | 16 to 4096, 256 by default. The dialog shows how much is in use. |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | Opens the shortcut editor ([Shortcuts](Shortcuts)). |
